The method 03

Don't start with the chart. Start with the question.

01

Question

Which behaviour are we trying to understand, and why could it matter in the match?

02

Evidence

Select metrics and visuals that genuinely add information instead of showing data for its own sake.

03

Story

Connect the patterns to the tactical reading, separating what the data shows from what it only suggests.
